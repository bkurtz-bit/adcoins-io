The network

An audience built on intent, not on a list purchase.

Most “finance” audiences are demographic guesses resold between data brokers. Ours is behavioural: users enter the pool by interacting with financial and trading content inside our partner network, and we pay to remove everything that doesn’t look like a real prospect.

Demonstrated financial intent

Pool membership requires a click on trading, exchange or financial-services content — a much stronger signal than an interest category.

Filtered, not just collected

Over $3M spent screening traffic from the large networks so bot and low-quality inventory never reaches your campaign.

Targeting beyond geography

Keywords, site placements, topics, device, age, gender and estimated income — layered on top of 200+ country targeting.

How retargeting works

Four steps from anonymous visitor to funded account.

01 — User enters the pool

Someone visits a finance or trading site inside our network. That behaviour puts them into the retargeting audience.

02 — They leave, but stay tracked

The user moves on. They remain eligible for remarketing across thousands of partner sites for the duration of the window.

03 — Your ad follows

Your banner, HTML5 or video creative appears wherever that user browses next, filtered by your geo and targeting rules.

04 — Conversions are measured

Our tracking code fires on your site so the report shows conversions, not just impressions and clicks.
